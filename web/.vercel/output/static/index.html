Homebrew, actually visible

BrewMenu lives in your menu bar and tells you what Homebrew never does: updates, warnings, and cleanups, the moment they matter. Without touching the terminal.

View on GitHub

New Mac, no Homebrew yet? This one-liner installs both.

Free and open source. No account, no sign-up.

Somewhere, right now:

  • 9 packages quietly out of date
  • A brew doctor warning you've never actually seen
  • 1.4 GB of Cellar cruft nobody's reclaimed
  • A background service that's been down since this morning

Homebrew doesn't tell you any of this. It just sits in the terminal, waiting to be asked.

Right where you'd actually look: next to your clock, wifi, and battery.

Not just updates. The whole picture.

Warnings you'd never go looking for

Insights

Watches your setup over time and tells you what's gone stale, what's worth cleaning, what needs attention today.

Install Packs

Dev Essentials, Web Dev, DevOps, AI Dev Tools: curated bundles instead of typing brew install one at a time.

Universal search

Every formula, cask, and tap in one field. Type a bare name or a whole tap, BrewMenu figures out what you meant.

Need more than a glance? Open the Dashboard.

One click from the menu bar, or ⌘D, and every formula, pack, and outdated package is right there.

Nothing but Homebrew

Reads and manages your Cellar only. No access to the rest of your Mac.

Native SwiftUI

No Electron. No background daemon quietly draining your battery.

Open source

MIT licensed, on GitHub, for anyone to read or contribute to.

macOS 14+

Built and tested on Sonoma and later. Older systems aren't supported yet.

Two minutes to install. One command to remove.

Uninstall anytime with brew uninstall --cask dotfn/tap/brewmenu. Nothing left behind.
